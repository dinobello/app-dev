# Snowfall

## About the Series
**Snowfall** is an American crime drama television series that premiered in 2017. It was created by John Singleton, Eric Amadio, and Dave Andron. The series is set in Los Angeles during the 1980s and follows the rise of the crack cocaine epidemic and its effects on different people and communities.

## Main Character
**Franklin Saint** – A young and ambitious man from South Central Los Angeles who becomes involved in the drug business. As the story progresses, he faces difficult decisions that affect his family, relationships, and future.

## Main Cast
- Damson Idris as Franklin Saint

- Sergio Peris-Mencheta as Gustavo Zapata
- Michael Hyatt as Cissy Saint

## Genre
- Crime
- Drama
- Thriller

## Setting
The story takes place mainly in Los Angeles, California, during the 1980s. It shows the social and economic struggles of the community during the crack cocaine epidemic.

## What Makes the Series Interesting?
Snowfall shows how money, power, ambition, and difficult choices can change a person's life. It also explores the effects of the drug trade on families, friendships, and the community. The different storylines make the series interesting because they show how the characters' lives are connected.

## Personal Takeaway
For me, Snowfall is an interesting series because it shows that every decision has consequences. It also highlights how ambition and the desire for success can lead people into situations that are difficult to escape.
